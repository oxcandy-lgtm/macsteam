# Trademark Policy — MacSteam Ultimate

> **Version:** 1.0  
> **Applies to:** MacSteam Ultimate (cloverpit-u1)

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how MacSteam may refer to third-party trademarks, what constitutes acceptable use, and what uses are prohibited. It does **not** grant any licence to third-party marks; it documents boundaries.

---

## 2. Our Mark: "MacSteam"

- "MacSteam" is an **independent project name** and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Valve Corporation, Steam, or any other entity.**
- MacSteam's name is used in a **nominative, descriptive sense** — to identify a Mac-native frontend for managing Windows games via compatibility layers.
- The project claims no trademark ownership over "MacSteam" beyond any common-law rights that may arise from use in commerce. The name was chosen to indicate the project's purpose (Steam-integration on Mac) and is not intended to create confusion with Valve's marks.

### Acceptable Use of "MacSteam"
- ✅ Refer to the project and its software.
- ✅ Use in documentation, articles, and reviews describing the project.
- ❌ Registering "MacSteam" as a trademark or domain that implies official association with Valve or Steam.
- ❌ Using "MacSteam" in a way that suggests it is an official Valve product.

## 4. Logo Usage

- MacSteam **does not include** third-party logos, icons, or branding in its own UI or installer graphics.
- References to third-party products in text (documentation, README, in-app labels) are made using plain text only — no logo, no stylised mark.
- The MacSteam project uses its own original iconography. No third-party icon is included in the app bundle.
